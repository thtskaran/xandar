MODEL BOUNDARIES

Evidence is not certainty.

Static-supported means a source reference supports a component or conditional relationship. Observed means a normal browser request was recorded. Request order alone never establishes causality. Inferred means a curated relationship needs owner review.

Availability, integrity and confidentiality follow different directions and preconditions. No reachability result proves exploitation, data exposure, actual downtime or a verified vulnerability. No scanner, provider call or fault injection runs from this page.
