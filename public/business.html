WORKSPACE / APPLICATION CONTEXT

Application facts

Juice Shop brings the storefront, its recorded behavior and business context into one reviewable workspace.

The application

OWASP Juice Shop is a deliberately vulnerable shopping application running locally. Its logical components belong to one application; they are not separately deployed microservices.

The customer journey

Customers browse products, sign in, manage a basket and place orders. Recorded requests show only the interactions actually visited. Missing or unreadable evidence remains a gap.

The business context

Policies, orders, products and customer records provide a source-linked business reference. Historical revenue is context, not profit, predicted loss or a measure of affected customers.

The analysis boundary

Analysis uses available source and ordinary traffic evidence. Model assessments are not exploit validation. Downstream consequences depend on explicit conditions; forecasts require reviewed assumptions.
